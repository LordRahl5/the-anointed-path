Featured Project

The Anointed Path

A mythic dark fantasy universe built for games, film, and cinematic storytelling.

The Anointed Path is a large-scale fantasy world shaped by prophecy, betrayal, celestial war, fallen beings, ancient kingdoms, and emotional character journeys.

The universe follows Ezrael, Seraphina, Thalios, and other key figures as they move through realms of corruption, divine calling, war, and redemption.

About The Studio

Built for mythic storytelling.

Crown & Cross Studios is an independent cinematic game and entertainment studio focused on mythic worlds, dark fantasy stories, emotionally driven characters, and high-impact visual design.

The studio is developing The Anointed Path as a connected universe for games, cinematic trailers, music, books, and long-form storytelling.
